# Export Data Visualization & Trend Analysis (2016–2022)

**Project Type:** Excel Data Analysis & Visualization  
**Tools:** Microsoft Excel  
**Dataset Scope:** Export data for West Java, Indonesia, 2016–2022

## Project Overview

This case study analyzes export values by product category and year. The workbook transforms raw records into ranked summaries, percentage contributions, trend analysis, and supporting visualizations to make the export data easier to interpret.

## Analysis Workflow

1. Summarized total export value for each product category and ranked categories from highest to lowest.
2. Identified the top 10 product categories by cumulative export value.
3. Created a horizontal bar chart with ascending order for easier comparison.
4. Calculated each top-10 category's contribution to total export value.
5. Analyzed the annual export trend for the Paper category, including year-over-year growth and CAGR.
6. Prepared a regional summary of export value and order quantity, while flagging unavailable profit data.
7. Examined annual export movements and the product categories contributing most to increases and decreases.

## Selected Findings

- Total export value recorded in the dataset for 2016–2022 was **USD 214.27 billion**.
- The top 10 product categories contributed approximately **70.2%** of total export value.
- Vehicles Other Than Trains and Electrical Equipments together contributed approximately **28.5%** of total export value.
- Paper export value increased from approximately **USD 844.99 million in 2016** to **USD 1.19 billion in 2022**, with a reported CAGR of approximately **5.94%**.
- Total annual export value declined in 2019 and 2020, then increased in 2021 and 2022. The strongest year-over-year increase in the period was approximately **27.3% in 2021**.
- The workbook reports a correlation of approximately **0.005** between export value and order quantity in the raw data, indicating almost no linear relationship in this dataset.

## Visualization & Excel Techniques

- Ranked summary tables
- Top-10 horizontal bar chart
- Percentage contribution analysis
- Annual line chart for Paper exports
- Annual export trend chart
- SUMIFS, LARGE, INDEX/MATCH, year-over-year growth, CAGR, and correlation calculations

## Data Limitations

- The dataset contains export records for **West Java only**, so the findings should not be generalized to other regions.
- The data is annual. Monthly or quarterly patterns cannot be assessed.
- The source does not contain cost or profit fields. Therefore, profit cannot be calculated reliably; export value is used as the value measure.
- External factors such as global demand, exchange rates, trade policy, and the COVID-19 pandemic are contextual explanations, not variables directly tested by this dataset.
- Correlation is descriptive and does not establish causation.
